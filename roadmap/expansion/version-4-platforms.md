# V4 — Alternative TV platforms (research-gated)

[Русский](../../docs/ru/roadmap/expansion/version-4-platforms.md)

Research Tizen and webOS application models, signing, app-store distribution, background networking, web runtime and URI opening before committing to implementation. Build a compatibility matrix, spike prototypes and record ADRs. No shared implementation abstraction until a second real platform proves common semantics. Each receiver advertises `capabilities` rather than claiming parity. Apple TV and Cast require platform-specific strategies and may not allow arbitrary browser navigation.

## Gate
Document a tested, installable URL-receiving proof of concept on each target platform before adding it to product marketing or roadmap as committed.
