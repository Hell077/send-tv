# Landing copy (English draft)

## Hero
**A link on your phone. A page on your TV.**
Send URLs from any device to your Android TV over your local network. No accounts. No cloud relay.

Primary CTA: `Get started` | Secondary CTA: `Read the docs`

## How it works
1. Install receiver on Android TV.
2. Scan QR code and approve pairing.
3. Paste a link and open it on your TV.

## Features
- URL handoff, not unreliable screen recording.
- Device approval and revoke access.
- Local-first by design.
- CLI/discovery marked `Planned` until released.

## Honest compatibility
V1 requires a supported Android/Google TV receiver app and a browser or compatible URL handler. Not all websites work well with a remote control. Tizen/webOS, file transfer and mirroring are future research, not current features.

## Privacy
Links are sent to your local receiver; the destination site loads directly on the TV. The LAN control channel may initially use HTTP; do not share sensitive links over untrusted Wi-Fi.
